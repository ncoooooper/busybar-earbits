# EarBits

Train your ear on four ascending intervals with original two-note recordings and a persistent score.

Hear a pair of notes before you answer. The front leaves the choice in view while the back keeps your accuracy and best streak. You can replay a pair as often as you need without using up a question.

## Listen again before committing

1. Start a question and listen to the two notes in order. The first is your reference.
2. Press **Start** to hear the same two notes again. The current answer stays on screen while you listen.
3. Turn the wheel to choose Major 3rd, Perfect 4th, Perfect 5th or Octave. Turning changes your answer; only a click grades it.

![The selected interval changes from Major 3rd to Perfect 4th when the wheel turns](media/guide/replay-and-choose.gif)

## Grade a guess and start another pair

1. Click the wheel when you have picked an interval. In this run the player chose **Perfect 4th**.
2. Read the feedback. **Hear again** marks the missed answer in this clip. Compare your accuracy and streak on the back.
3. Click after the feedback to hear a different question. Use Start during that question if you need another listen.

![The selected Perfect 4th changes to Hear again after the wheel click](media/guide/grade-the-interval.gif)

## Practising with sound

The GIFs show the controls and feedback. To practise the interval itself, turn on the bar’s sound or enable audio in the emulator. Each exercise uses a recorded ascending pair. The app stores the running score locally, so reopening it does not discard your practice history.

## Run in the emulator

Open BUSY Bar Emulator. From this repository, run:

```sh
~/.busy-emulator/bin/busy-emulator dev --once
```

Press **Back** to exit.

The GIFs are excerpts from the emulator recording. The front display is enlarged 9× and the end frames pause for reading.
